NO cute polished vintage storybook, no engraving finish, no ornamental backgrounds, no sepia wash, no smooth digital rendering. ## 12 — Contratos com o impossível

Use case: illustration-story. NEW concept sheet of SIX equal square cards in exact 3 columns x 2 rows. Use reference ONLY as raw drawing style. Adult surrealist black felt-tip and scratchy pen drawings, rough sparse awkward human bodies, near-blank faces, huge white negative space, strange bold black masses, dry social absurdism, no detailed backgrounds, no beautiful vintage illustration, no cosy or cute mood, no sepia, no collage, no digital gradients. Rare flat accents: small vermilion, petrol-green, mustard objects, each accent carries the action. Six disruptive fictional story seeds about exchange, invisible work, ownership and future possibilities. Exact scenes: TOP LEFT an adult bureaucrat behind a very small desk removes a RED physical speech-bubble-shaped stone from a visitor's outstretched hands and places it in a deep open drawer; the visitor holds an empty oval outline like a receipt in the other hand, no writing, open unresolved transaction, not medical injury. TOP MIDDLE on otherwise blank ground a person in a coat carefully unrolls a MUSTARD strip of sunlight like a measuring tape to mark a queue; three adults wait INSIDE the thin strip while the huge white space on either side is entirely free. TOP RIGHT a very small plain hovering delivery ROBOT carries one end of a red paper carpet toward a flat solid-black wall; two ordinary adults fold the carpet's far end up against the wall into the outline of a door, visibly making a passage from a delivery rather than using a built door. BOTTOM LEFT two adults seated at opposite ends of one simple table quietly exchange huge green GLOVES; one glove contains a tiny miniature human doing household work at a tiny sink, clearly a surreal symbolic prop with no harm or captivity violence; the outside adults look at the work not each other, story about invisible effort left open. BOTTOM MIDDLE a tenant carries an entire ordinary EMPTY window frame by its handle like a briefcase; trailing behind it is a narrow black ribbon of night, while a second adult holds up a tiny orange sunrise-shaped paper cutout as if offering it in exchange; no landscapes. BOTTOM RIGHT two adults have laid a ladder FLAT between them on white floor, each stands at one end looking toward the other; between the rungs a third person's long black SHADOW is calmly climbing sideways, a single mustard key lies beside one end; no hugging, no fixed resolution. Make every action legible in spare line art, mysterious rather than explanatory; no labels, words, letters, digits, signs or speech text. No gore, no injury, no helpless-victim caricature, adults only. Exactly SIX panels. Each panel can open or complicate multiple stories, not a moral lesson.
