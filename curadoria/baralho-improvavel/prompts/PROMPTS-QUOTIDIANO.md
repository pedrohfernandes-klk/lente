## 27 — Cheguei, mas fiquei lá

Use case illustration-story. Create ONE new SIX-card sheet exact 3 columns by 2 rows equal square panels. Reference ONLY for raw spare style, invent all scenes. Theme I CAME HOME BUT PART OF ME STAYED AT WORK: recognizable adult everyday moments given ONE surprising surreal twist. Emotion through gesture and concrete objects, not abstract puzzles. Rough scratchy black fineliner and uneven marker on pure WHITE, awkward adult human figures with nearly blank faces, strong simple black shapes, at least 65 percent empty white, ONE small flat red/mustard/petrol-green accent per scene. No beautiful vintage engraving, ornamental scenery, sepia texture, cinematic shading, cute faces, gradients or polished illustration. Six separate open narrative situations: TOP LEFT adult arriving home carrying an ordinary briefcase; their long black SHADOW stays seated at a tiny OFFICE DESK behind them typing on a blank old typewriter while the actual body sets down a small GREEN dinner plate on a plain kitchen table, only essential furniture. TOP MIDDLE two adult flatmates face a tall laundry pile on a small table; one pulls a RED shirt sleeve that continues impossibly around the other person's wrist like a long loose ribbon, both hold ordinary laundry without injury, unresolved division of work, neither gender-coded as servant. TOP RIGHT two adult friends at a cafe table each holds one end of a long MUSTARD receipt-like blank paper strip; it has become a little bridge between their ordinary cups, one friend has a hand on an unopened wallet, no letters or price digits. BOTTOM LEFT an adult in a work coat tries to fit an enormous BLACK CLOCK-shaped bag through a tiny ordinary doorway while another adult already inside offers a small RED cup; clock has two hands no numbers, no happy reunion predetermined. BOTTOM MIDDLE two adults preparing a meal at opposite sides of a plain counter; one carefully chops a small BLACK cloud into pieces on a board while the other holds a GREEN bowl beneath its drips, familiar shared chore with uncanny material, knife only on board, no harm. BOTTOM RIGHT two adult siblings on a plain bench each holds an ordinary childhood-size shoe; between them a very long BLACK distance unrolls from an open small cardboard shoebox across the white floor, one tiny red shoelace, no sentimental smiles. All people unmistakably adults, varied pairings incl two women and two men as well as mixed pairs, no gender stereotype of who cares or works. Human tenderness, friction, fatigue, money and memory through dry absurdity; images usable in different narrative orders with causes left open. No writing, words, lettering, speech bubbles, symbols as labels, digits or watermarks. Exactly SIX panels.

## 28 — Quase combinámos

Use case illustration-story. NEW sheet SIX square narrative cards in exact 3 columns by 2 rows. Same raw adult surrealist line-art zine as reference, but emotionally relatable ordinary moments: friends trying to meet, a difficult call, asking for help, arriving at someone's door, time that does not match. Scratchy uneven BLACK pen, strong few flat-black shapes, huge untextured WHITE space, awkward body language with near-blank faces, VERY small isolated vermilion/mustard/petrol-green accent. No polished vintage prettiness, fancy background, gradients, cute sentimentality, moral poster, explanatory text or speech bubbles. Original concrete actions, clear familiar object and only ONE surreal twist each. All people adult. EXACT SIX images: TOP LEFT two adult WOMEN in ordinary casual clothes stand over a plain cafe table, each unrolls a long strip of WHITE calendar-like paper with black rectangular blocks NO numbers; only a tiny MUSTARD patch of free white space overlaps between their strips, both point to it rather than smile.
