## 06 — A rua entre nós

Use case: illustration-story. ONE original narrative game sheet of SIX equal square panels, precise 3 columns x 2 rows grid. Setting THE STREET BETWEEN US, an understated retro-future city. Make it visually striking but HUMAN, awkward, questioning, dry adult zine humour, not a cute moral fable. Hand-drawn black PEN LINE ART, angular confident outlines, sparse hatching, generous untextured ivory space, tiny flat rusty-orange and petrol-green accents, simple legible silhouettes. No collage, neon, glossy sci-fi, gradients, cinematic photorealism or ornamental sepia texture. Mix a 1950s tram-stop clock, rotary telephone and old municipal chairs with a plain transparent access panel and an unusual small hovering delivery tray. NO letters, numbers, readable writing, icons shaped like written characters, speech balloons or captions. Recurring adults: woman with short grey hair, practical long orange coat, carrying a battered green toolbox; younger adult man with dense short curly black hair, green work jacket and plain messenger bag. Both have purpose and agency, no caricatured vulnerability, restrained alert expressions, neither is saviour. SAME narrow dark fissure divides a public street in all images; it is a metaphorical spatial obstacle, no one falls or is hurt. SIX CARDS represent situations and THREE alternative outcomes, NOT a single mandatory happy ending. TOP LEFT wide street view: both adults face each other across the fissure beside an old tram-stop shelter; a sleek transparent CLOSED access gate sits along the gap, no text. TOP MIDDLE medium view: the woman holds the receiver of a rotary phone on one side while the man on other side holds an earpiece from a small futuristic wireless unit; neither device is connected to the other, each holds the free end visibly, they look puzzled, no cartoon bubbles. TOP RIGHT close medium: gate's small round sensor reads only a hovering EMPTY delivery tray while the two adults stand beside it with toolboxes, suggesting the system sees an object but has not admitted people; use simple small circle on sensor, no green/red judgement or words. BOTTOM LEFT possible ending A: the two adults have placed a long old wooden tabletop ACROSS the fissure and work together repairing the rotary phone, each remains on their own side, makeshift shared work surface rather than hugging. BOTTOM MIDDLE possible ending B: each adult stays on their own side, exchanging a small envelope via a basket suspended from a simple taut cord over the gap; they choose distance while maintaining contact, ordinary dignified gestures. BOTTOM RIGHT possible ending C: transparent gate now open and hovering tray parked idle, woman starts across but man has turned and is walking away holding his bag, both visible; no villain, reunion not guaranteed, open ambiguous interpretation. Equal graphic weight to all outcomes, same street and objects repeated consistently, human scale. Make actions concrete and uncluttered. No labels or panel numbers.

Recurring neighbour B: younger adult woman with dark skin indicated by restrained ink hatching, short tightly curled hair, green overalls and a small compact projector; show both as capable people with interests, not opposites good vs bad. TOP LEFT: neighbours face one another from adjacent balconies, separated by a clear sliding panel with a large simple OPEN-HAND silhouette stuck on it; one reads it as greeting and raises a hand, other stops reaching and pauses, interpretation visibly different yet not exaggerated. TOP MIDDLE: man inside his room listening to old radio while woman next door looks at palm-sized projector showing a plain empty ring; both devices emit small differently shaped graphic sound strokes ONLY, absolutely no text. TOP RIGHT: medium view at separating transparent panel, man offers an ordinary sealed paper envelope through a small opening while woman holds the projector aside and looks closely at his gesture; the hand silhouette on panel stays visible, unresolved. BOTTOM LEFT alternative A: both neighbours seated on either side of a narrow shared table at the threshold, drawing their own different simple hand/circle symbols on separate paper then placing the papers side by side; they compare instead of one teaching the other, no written words, no smiley emoji. BOTTOM MIDDLE alternative B: man keeps his room's curtain partly closed, woman leaves a sealed envelope on a shared shelf outside it, then turns to return to her own work; chosen privacy coexists with possible contact, no evil silhouette. BOTTOM RIGHT alternative C: transparent panel is fully open; man has put his radio on one small chair and woman her projector on a second chair facing it, both people themselves sit slightly apart looking out toward blank open sky; a human quiet pause, no forced handshake or embrace. Use SAME people, clothing and panel motif all six cards, clear actions, minimal architecture. Outcomes equal visual weight; they are prompts for stories, not labelled correct/incorrect. Draw no readable text anywhere.

All details invented for a hypothetical story; no real people, institution, nation, logos, badges, barcodes or identity documents. Minimal background, strong readable focal action, same characters and object designs throughout. Draw EXACTLY SIX panels.
